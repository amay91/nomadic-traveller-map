    About this map

    A personal map of every country you've been to, and when. Click a country or type its name, add the year or years you were there, and mark whether you visited, lived there, or call it home. The map fills in as you go.

    Your map is the link. Everything you record is encoded into this page's own address — there's no account to create, no server storing your travel history, and nothing about you leaving your device. Bookmark the link and you've saved your map; email it to yourself and you've backed it up; open it anywhere and it's there.

    It works without a network. Add it to your home screen and it opens and runs offline, which is the point for something you might reach for on a plane or in a hotel with bad wifi.

    The geography is honest. The map uses an Equal Earth projection, where every country's drawn area is proportional to its real area. The familiar school-room world map inflates everything near the poles — Greenland appears roughly the size of Africa, when Africa is fourteen times larger. On a map built to compare where you've been, that would quietly flatter some journeys and shrink others.

    195 countries, plus 45 territories kept separate. Greenland, Taiwan, Puerto Rico and others are real, distinct, inhabited places worth recording — but they aren't sovereign countries, so they're tracked without ever inflating your count out of 195. The pill in the corner explains the distinction.

    Built as a small, dependency-free web page: no framework, no tracking, no analytics, nothing to install. Source on GitHub.

    Your map lives in its own link — no account, nothing stored on a server. Email it to yourself to keep it.

    ·

    195 is the official count — UN member states plus the Holy See and the State of Palestine (Worldometers/UN). The counter above tracks only these.

    45 more territories are also shown and clickable — real, distinct, inhabited places (Greenland, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Puerto Rico, …) that aren't sovereign countries. They're tracked too, just kept separate from "of 195."
